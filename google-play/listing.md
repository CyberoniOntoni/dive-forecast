## Full description (max 4000)

```
50Bar forecasts the current at dive sites across the Maldives: which way the water runs, how strong it is, and how sure the forecast is, hour by hour for about a week.

Channel dives in the Maldives are planned around the current. An incoming current brings the reef sharks and mantas to the channel mouth; an outgoing one can push you out into the blue. 50Bar puts that answer on one screen for over 300 sites, from North Malé and Ari to the southern atolls.

THE MAP
• Every site shows an arrow pointing the way the water runs this hour, coloured by incoming, outgoing or along the reef
• Counts of sites running very strong, strong, mild and slack right now; tap one to show only those sites
• Switch to a list of the sites in an atoll, strongest first
• Satellite or street map, with reef outlines, reef zones, bottom types, depth shading and contours as overlays
• Sheltered water: sea state across each atoll and the sea around it, hour by hour

EACH SITE
• Strength (slack, mild, strong, very strong), direction and a confidence level for the hour
• The tide: rising or falling, the next high or low, and when the flow next changes
• A 24-hour chart for each day of the week, with flow bars and the tide line; drag to any hour
• Sea, swell and wind at the site
• Depths, notes on how to dive it, photos and reviews from other divers
• Share the hour as a link

HOW THE FORECAST WORKS
50Bar combines the tide, the water level difference across the atoll and the monsoon current running through it, using open ocean data, and adjusts each site with reports from divers who dived there. Each site tells you what the forecast is built from and how confident it is. The forecast is a planning aid: always follow your guide and dive centre, and check conditions on site.

REPORT WHAT YOU FOUND
Sign in with your email (we send a code, no password) and report the current after a dive in a couple of taps: "as forecast", or which way and how strong it was. Reports make the next forecast at that site better for everyone. You can also rate sites, write reviews with photos, and add sites that are missing.

DIVE PLANS
Guides and liveaboard crews can plan a trip day by day, in the app or on the web: each dive is checked against the forecast at its time, with warnings for very strong or wrong-way current and a better window when there is one. Plans are kept on the phone for use out of signal.

BOAT NAVIGATION
Head to any site or point on the map: 50Bar draws a route around the islands and reefs and gives a steer that allows for the current, with distance and arrival time. It uses satellite reef outlines, not a nautical chart, so keep a lookout.

WORKS OFFLINE
Save an atoll before you leave the jetty: forecasts, site pages, reef shapes and satellite imagery stay on the phone for when there is no signal.

Free, with no ads. Reading forecasts needs no account. Your nickname is the only thing other divers see; your email is never shown. Privacy policy: https://50bar.app/privacy

Web version: https://50bar.app
```
